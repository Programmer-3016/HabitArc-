DATA HANDLING OVERVIEW · UPDATED NOVEMBER 21, 2026

Privacy & your data.

Beta notice: This overview describes the current implementation and must be reviewed and completed by the project owner before a public Play Store release. It is not yet a finalized legal privacy policy.

What you choose to save

HabitArc stores habit names, completion history, tasks, dates, preferences, notes (including optional photographs and voice recordings) and focus-session history so that the features you request can function.

Where your information is stored

Habit and task records have device-local copies and can be synchronized through Firebase for signed-in accounts when the required service and security rules are enabled. Notes use device-local IndexedDB; note text and optional media backup use Firebase Firestore and Cloud Storage when those services are configured and available. Focus history has a local copy and an optional signed-in Firebase backup. Some records may remain only on the current device while offline or when cloud setup is unavailable.

AI questions

When you explicitly submit a question to ARC AI, the content you send and relevant HabitArc context needed for the response may be transmitted to the HabitArc server and its configured AI service. The Daily Rescue Mode itself uses transparent local scheduling rules and does not automatically send your task list to AI.

Optional League & Friends Squads

Your habit streaks stay private unless you explicitly opt in to the Live League or a 6-character Friends Squad on the Leaderboard page. When opted in, only your chosen League display handle, avatar color tone, and aggregate points, streak, and clear-day counts are visible to signed-in League participants. Individual habit names, notes, tasks, and email addresses are never shared, and leaving the Live League deletes your public League card.

Notifications and permissions

Browser notification permission is optional. HabitArc's ordinary reminders work while the app is running and permitted. If you explicitly enable the optional background daily digest, this device's Web Push subscription endpoint and encrypted delivery keys are saved under your signed-in Firebase account so the server can send a daily notification even when the PWA is closed. The daily cron runs around 08:30–09:30 India time; exact delivery is not guaranteed. You can disable the subscription from Settings, and signing out attempts to unsubscribe the device. The browser and its push provider also process the push subscription. Photos and microphone access are used only when you choose those note features.

Backups and device changes

Settings offers a downloadable JSON archive with habits, tasks, notes, attachments and focus history, and a merge-only restore option for matching accounts. The archive is not encrypted. Store it somewhere private. Avoid clearing site storage or uninstalling the PWA before verifying your backup.

Deleting data or closing your account

You can delete individual notes, tasks and habits in the app. A complete self-service account deletion workflow, including verification of all Firebase data deletion, is not yet available in this beta. The project owner must provide a functioning deletion-request process before public Play Store publication.

What still needs review

Before public release, the owner must verify the actual service providers, retention periods, security configuration, support contact, deletion procedures and Play Console Data safety disclosures, and publish an approved policy with contact details.
