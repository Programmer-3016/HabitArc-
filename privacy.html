HabitArc Privacy & Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Applies to HabitArc Web App & Installable PWA

Privacy at a glance: HabitArc never sells personal data, never shows third-party ad trackers, and keeps your habits, planner tasks, focus timers, notes, and ARC AI chats private to your account. You can export or permanently delete your account and cloud data at any time from Settings.

1. Data We Collect & How It Is Stored

Category Examples Where Stored & Purpose
Account Identity Email address, display name, profile avatar URL (when signing in with Google or Email/Password) Firebase Authentication & Firestore user profile for multi-device sign-in and account recovery.
Habits, Planner & Focus Habit titles, schedules, completion timestamps, tasks, due dates, focus timer sessions, theme & week-start preferences Browser localStorage for offline speed and private Firestore subcollections under /users/{uid} when signed in.
Notes & Attachments Rich notes, checklists, and optional user-attached images or voice notes Browser IndexedDB offline store and private Firestore / Firebase Storage paths restricted to your authenticated UID.
ARC AI Coach Questions you submit, saved chat threads, and optional context items you explicitly attach Sent over HTTPS to HabitArc’s server endpoint (/api/coach) and processed via Google Gemini API solely to generate your coaching response. Saved threads sync only to your private account.
Push Reminders (Opt-In) Browser Web Push endpoint, encryption keys, and local timezone Stored under your private push-device record only when you explicitly enable Background Push in Settings.
Live League & Squads (Opt-In) Chosen public nickname (max 20 chars), avatar color tone, aggregate weekly/all-time points, streak count, and optional 6-character Squad code Published to /leagueMembers/{uid} only if you opt in. Private habit names, tasks, notes, and email addresses are never shared in League or Squads.

2. How Your Data Is Protected

  • Account isolation: Firestore and Firebase Storage security rules restrict every personal collection under /users/{uid} exclusively to your verified Firebase Authentication UID.
  • Explicit context control: Planner tasks, focus sessions, and notes are never sent to ARC AI unless you explicitly attach them or submit a prompt.
  • No ad networks or data brokers: HabitArc does not sell, rent, or share personal data for cross-app advertising.

3. Your Data Controls: Export, Backup & Opt-Out

  • CSV & Full JSON Backup: Open Settings → Export Data (CSV) or Download Full Backup to download a portable copy of your habits, completions, tasks, notes, media, and focus history.
  • Leave Live League: Open Rewards → League → Manage → Leave Live League at any time to remove your public nickname and points from the leaderboard.
  • Disable Push Notifications: Turn off reminders or click Disable on This Device in Settings to revoke Web Push delivery immediately.

4. Self-Service Account & Cloud Data Deletion

Permanent Deletion Inside the App: You can delete your HabitArc account and all associated cloud and local data at any time without emailing support:
  1. Sign in to HabitArc and open Settings.
  2. Scroll to Data Management and select Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  3. Type DELETE in the confirmation dialog and press Delete Permanently.

Confirming deletion immediately removes:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account login;
  • Your Firestore profile and all subcollections (habits, habitCompletions, tasks, notes, focusSessions, pushDevices, and saved arcChats);
  • Any note photos or audio files stored under users/{uid}/ in Firebase Storage;
  • Your opt-in Live League profile and Squad membership; and
  • All HabitArc local caches (localStorage, sessionStorage, and IndexedDB) on the active device.

If you only want to clear starter habits without deleting your login, use Reset App Data in Settings instead.
